## Script / protocol

**Warm-up:**
1. Tell me about the last time you {{context}}.

**Core:**
2. Walk me through what you did.
3. What was frustrating about that?
4. What did you do instead?

**Wrap:**
5. If you could change one thing, what?

> Ask about **past behavior, not future intent**. "Would you use this?" is the least
> reliable question in research. "When did you last try to do this?" is the most.
