> **Why ADRs.** A decision recorded in a Slack thread is gone in a week. In a PR
> comment, gone in a month. In `docs/decisions/`, it's still answering "why is it
> like this?" two years later — usually for the person who decided it.
>
> Write one for any choice that is expensive to reverse. Skip it for anything you
> could change on a Tuesday afternoon.
