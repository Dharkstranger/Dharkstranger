Who We Are

More than a party.
It's a tradition.

Definition of a Party — DOP — is an annual house party held every December 29 in the heart of Surulere, Lagos. Born from the streets that raised legends, DOP is a celebration of culture, community, and good energy.

Every year, we bring together friends, music lovers, and the Surulere community for a night that defines what a real party feels like. No gimmicks. Just vibes.
